# Alchemia MD: text, font and colour rules (Nov 2026)

Paste this into any chat that writes or designs for Alchemia MD.

## Fonts
| Role | Font | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Headlines (H1–H4) | **BC Novatica** (fallback Figtree) | Page titles, section headings, card titles. H1 semibold (600), others medium (500). |
| Body | **Dejanire Text** (fallback Newsreader / Georgia) | Paragraphs, descriptions, card text. |
| Callouts and buttons | **Roboto** | Labels, eyebrows, buttons, menu, badges, small headers, notes and disclaimers. |

## Text rules
- **No ALL CAPS anywhere.** Anything that used to be all caps is now **Roboto small caps**: type it in normal sentence case ("Start here", "Protect + Balance") and it displays as small caps. Do not type words in capitals, because capitals stay full size.
- Small-caps labels are bold (700) with light letter spacing (about 0.07–0.12em).
- **No numbers on steps, cards or lists** (no 01 / 02 / 03, no "Step 1"). Use the step name only.
- Section labels (eyebrows) sit above the heading in small caps. Section headers can be large (for example Our Mission, Brand Philosophy, Prevention on the About page).
- Science notes and disclaimers are set in **Roboto**, regular weight, in a card with a gold left border.
- Headlines: short, sentence case, ending with a full stop ("Balanced skin starts from within.").

## Buttons
- Primary button: **shiny gold** (#E3B04B gradient) with white small-caps text on dark or green backgrounds; black on beige or grey backgrounds.
- Vary the wording by destination: **Shop Now** (goes to buy), **Discover** (bundle cards), **Explore More** (product details), **Learn More** (quick views or articles). The floating corner button stays **Choose your plan**.

## Colours
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Black | #141414 | Hero, closing CTA, compliance notice |
| Charcoal | #212121 | Alternate dark section (one shade lighter than black) |
| Emerald | #132D22 | Main green |
| Lighter green | #24493A | Alternate green section |
| Gold | #C18C31 | Accents, borders, labels |
| Bright gold | #E3B04B | Buttons, announcement bar, glows |
| Beige (brand) | #F5F0E8 | Main light section |
| Warm beige | #DEBC8C | Occasional accent section (tan) |
| Grey (brand) | #E2DED8 | Light neutral section (FAQ, layers, About statements) |

## Section colour flow (follow this order down a page)
Black hero → Charcoal → Emerald → Lighter green → Beige content sections → Warm beige → Charcoal → Lighter green → Grey FAQ → Black closing CTA → compliance notice last.
- Never put two sections of the same colour next to each other unless they are meant to read as one block.
- The compliance notice (not sunscreen, consult your healthcare professional, FDA statement) is always the **very last section** on any product or bundle page.

## Compliance in copy
- No health or outcome claims; use "support", "supports normal…", "designed to".
- Add the dagger (†) to any structure/function statement.
- Always state that the supplements do not replace sunscreen or sun protection.
- Ingredient research is about individual ingredients, not the finished products.
