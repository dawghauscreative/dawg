# Alchemia MD: text, font and colour rules (updated Nov 20, Homepage 2 template)

Paste this into any chat that writes or designs for Alchemia MD. It replaces the Nov 2026 small-caps rules.

## Fonts
| Role | Font | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Headlines (H1–H4) | **BC Novatica** (fallback Figtree) | Page titles, section headings. Hero and key headings bold (700), other headings regular/medium. Sentence case. |
| Body | **Roboto** regular (400), light letter spacing | Paragraphs, descriptions, card text, quotes. |
| Labels, buttons, menu | **Roboto bold, ALL CAPS**, tracked (about 0.12–0.16em) | Eyebrows/labels, buttons, menu, footer links, product labels ("DAILY SUN SUPPORT"). |

## Text rules
- Labels, buttons, menu and footer links are **ALL CAPS** Roboto bold. Type them in normal case; the site capitalises them.
- Headlines are sentence case, never all caps, usually ending with a full stop.
- Product cards lead with a plain-language label in caps (Daily sun support, Daily immune support, Daily skin probiotic), then "Name | count".
- No numbers on steps or cards (no 01 / 02 / 03).

## Buttons
- **Flat gold pill** (#C18C31), white ALL CAPS text, on every background. Hover is a darker gold (#A87726). No metallic sheen.
- Wording: **Shop Now / Shop [product]** (buy), **Discover** (bundles), **Explore More** (details), **Learn More** (articles).

## Layout
- Photos have rounded corners (16px; 12px on small tiles). Product jar photos sit square on a light grey tile (#E0E0E0), on every page (product images use the same grey background).
- A short thick rule (grey #AFAFAF or tan #DEBC8C, about 155 × 6px) can sit above a section, top left.
- Footer: mailing list row (tan ALL CAPS label, pill email field with Subscribe), thin tan rule, four columns (Shop, Learn, Support, Legal) with tan headings and small caps links, FDA statement and copyright centred.
- Home page order: hero (jar on olive slope) → credit band → SPF statement → layered routine → activities → formula cards → first 90 days → founder note → mailing list / footer.

## Compliance in copy
- No health or outcome claims; use "support", "supports normal…", "designed to". No promises of visible change on a timeline (the 90-day section talks about the routine, not results).
- Add the dagger (†) to any structure/function statement.
- Always state that the supplements do not replace sunscreen or sun protection.
- Star ratings only from real Judge.me reviews; never placeholder counts.
- Free shipping threshold must match Shopify (currently $70).
