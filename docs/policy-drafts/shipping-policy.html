This page explains how and when Alchemia MD orders ship. Questions? Email info@dermalchemistmd.com.

Order processing

Orders ship from our fulfillment center in Colorado. They are typically processed and handed to the carrier within 1 to 3 business days (Monday to Friday, excluding holidays). When your order ships, we email you a confirmation with tracking information. Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed the next business day.

United States shipping

  • Standard shipping: $8.00 on orders under $70. Free on orders of $70 or more. Typically arrives in 3 to 7 business days after shipping.
  • Express shipping: $15.00. Typically arrives in 1 to 3 business days after shipping.

Shipping charges and your delivery options are shown at checkout before you pay.

International shipping

We ship to the following countries and regions: Australia, Austria, Belgium, Canada, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Hong Kong, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Japan, Malaysia, the Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Singapore, South Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, the United Arab Emirates and the United Kingdom.

International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on your destination and the weight of your order. Delivery times vary by destination and carrier.

Duties and taxes. International orders may be subject to import duties, taxes and customs fees charged by the destination country. These charges are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in our prices or shipping fees.

Delivery estimates

Delivery times are estimates and are not guaranteed. Delays can happen because of carrier issues, weather, customs processing or other events outside our control. Please use the tracking link in your shipping confirmation to follow your order.

Shipping address

Please check your shipping address before you place your order. We cannot change the address once an order has shipped. If your order is returned to us because of an incorrect or incomplete address, we will contact you about reshipping, and additional shipping charges may apply.

Lost, damaged or missing orders

If your tracking shows your order as delivered but you have not received it, or if your order arrives damaged or incomplete, please email info@dermalchemistmd.com with your order number as soon as possible so we can investigate and make it right. See our Refund policy for details on damaged or incorrect items.

Subscriptions

Subscription orders ship on the schedule you choose. Shipping charges and free-shipping eligibility for subscription renewals follow the rates above and are shown at checkout.
