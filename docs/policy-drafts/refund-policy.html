Returns
You have 30 days after your order is delivered to request a return. To be eligible, the product must be unopened and unused, in its original packaging. You will also need your order number or proof of purchase.

To start a return, email us at info@dermalchemistmd.com with your order number. If your return is accepted, we will send you a return shipping label and instructions. Items sent back without first requesting a return cannot be accepted.

Opened products
For safety and quality reasons, dietary supplements that have been opened or used cannot be returned. If you have a concern about a product you have opened, please contact us and we will review it with you.

Damaged, defective or incorrect items
Please inspect your order when it arrives. If an item is damaged, defective or not what you ordered, email info@dermalchemistmd.com within 14 days of delivery, with your order number and a photo if possible. We will send a replacement or issue a refund.

Items that cannot be returned
Gift cards and items marked final sale cannot be returned.

Exchanges
The fastest way to get a different product is to return the original item, and once the return is accepted, place a new order for the product you want.

Subscriptions
You can change, skip or cancel a subscription at any time from your account or the Manage subscription link, before your next renewal. See our Cancellations policy for details. Questions about a renewal charge? Email us and we will help.

European Union 14-day cooling-off period
If your order is shipped to the European Union, you have the right to cancel and return it within 14 days of delivery, for any reason and without giving a justification. As above, the product must be unopened and in its original packaging.

Refunds
Once we receive and inspect your return, we will email you to let you know whether the refund is approved. Approved refunds go to your original payment method within 10 business days. Your bank or card company may need additional time to post the refund. If more than 15 business days have passed since your return was approved, please contact us at info@dermalchemistmd.com.

Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the return is due to our error.
